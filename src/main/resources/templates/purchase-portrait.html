Purchase Order Agreement

This Purchase Order Agreement forms the complete production and supply instruction for {order.number}. By signing this document, confirming it in writing, or commencing production, the Supplier accepts the product lines, reference images, product instructions and terms below. A specific written product instruction takes precedence over a general term.

01Approved specification only

No substitution or change in material, colour, dimensions, construction, finish, scent, packaging, EAN, carton quantity or labelling is permitted without ENROSED's prior written approval.

02Quality and 1% maximum tolerance

Products must be new, clean, saleable and compliant with every instruction and reference image. ENROSED accepts no more than 1% damaged, defective, incomplete or non-conforming units per SKU. Any amount above 1% must, at ENROSED's choice, be replaced or credited by the Supplier.

03Quantity and packing accuracy

Ordered quantities, pieces per carton, carton dimensions and CBM must be checked before production. Short shipment, over shipment, mixed packing, unauthorised split shipment or incorrect carton marks require prior written approval. The Supplier remains responsible for resulting reasonable direct costs.

04Inspection and remedy

ENROSED may inspect before shipment and after receipt. Payment, inspection or receipt does not waive hidden defects. A claim may be supported by counts, photographs or video. The agreed remedy must be completed promptly and without disrupting the next shipment.
05Shipping schedule and deviations

The Supplier must immediately report any risk to production completion, booking, departure, route or arrival. No shipment may leave earlier, later, partially or by a different route or mode without ENROSED's written approval. Unauthorised deviations remain for the Supplier's account.

06Pre-shipment confirmation

Before release, the Supplier must provide final carton count, gross and net weight, measurements, CBM, packing list and clear production and packing photographs. Commercial invoice and labels must match this PO.

07Safe packing and transport protection

The Supplier must use export-worthy packing suitable for the agreed transport and protect the goods against pressure, moisture, movement, breakage and contamination. Approval of a packing photo does not release the Supplier from responsibility for inadequate packing.

08Written changes and records

Only changes confirmed in writing by ENROSED are valid. The Supplier must retain the approved samples, specifications and production records needed to trace any deviation. English text in this agreement governs the supplier-facing order.
